Avalution

Sponsor: Ava Labs
Team: Adyah Rastogi, Wesley Truong, Justin Lang, Hao Lee, Jiahua Ren



Mentor(s): Aaron Buchwald
Project Overview: Our project seeks to enhance the underlying architecture of the Avalanche blockchain by optimizing how key-value pairs of Merkle trees are stored directly on disk, moving beyond conventional database solutions like LMDB, LevelDB, and RocksDB. By developing and refining critical components such as the disk manager, free list, and revision manager, we aim to streamline the storage of recent Merkleized blockchain states, significantly reducing overhead and improving efficiency in state management.
